# Memorandum of Understanding: college and Poolit (DRAFT template)

*Between [COLLEGE / HOSTEL ADMINISTRATION] and [COMPANY NAME]. Date: [DATE]. Non-binding unless the lawyer makes parts binding.*

## Why this exists
Students in the hostels order snacks and essentials from the store in or near their hostel. Poolit groups the orders of one hostel into short pools so the store delivers a batch together, which lowers the delivery cost per student and the number of trips at the gate.

## What Poolit does
- Runs the student app and the store app; handles ordering, pooling and records.
- Signs up only stores that show a valid FSSAI licence, and keeps copies.
- Keeps student data limited to what delivery needs, and follows its Privacy Policy. Students can export or delete their data.
- Handles complaints within 48 hours (acknowledge) and one month (resolve).

## What the stores do
- Deliver by their own staff to the student's block and room, only to the student who ordered.
- Follow the college's security and visitor rules at the gate and in the hostel.

## What we ask of the college / hostel
1. Agree which stores may operate and deliver, and which gate or entry route they use.
2. Tell Poolit of rules on delivery hours, visitors, and items not allowed.
3. Name a contact person for hostel-level questions.
4. [Optional: a place or notice board where the app can be announced.]

## Safety and conduct
Delivery staff carry an ID the college accepts, deliver only within [hours], and do not enter rooms unless the hostel allows. Poolit and the store act at once on a complaint and stop a store or a person the college asks to stop, with reasons given.

## Money
Poolit pays the college nothing and the college charges Poolit nothing under this MoU. [Or: describe any fee or revenue share.]

## Data
The college receives no student data from Poolit except as a student gives it themselves or as the law requires. Aggregate numbers (orders per hostel) may be shared.

## Term
[N months], renewable. Either side can end it with [30] days' notice, or at once if safety is at risk.
