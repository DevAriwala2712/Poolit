## Facts this relies on
- Poolit is a platform. The goods are sold by the store in your hostel (the "Store"); the Store's staff deliver them. Poolit does not sell or deliver the goods itself.
- Orders in a hostel are grouped into a "pool" for a short window (set by the Store, about 10 minutes). The delivery fee each student pays depends on how many orders are in the pool when it closes: 1 to 2 orders ₹20, 3 to 5 ₹10, 6 to 10 ₹5, 11 or more free. A platform fee (currently ₹4) and an optional tip are added.
- You can pay by cash on delivery now; UPI is planned. Cash is handed to the Store's staff at the door.
- You sign in with your mobile number and a code sent by SMS. The app is for people aged 18 or over.

## 1. Who can use Poolit
You must be 18 or older and a resident of a hostel where Poolit operates. By signing in you confirm both. We may suspend accounts that are used by someone else, or that give false details.

## 2. Your account
Keep your phone safe: anyone with your phone can place orders in your name. Tell us at once if it is lost. Give your real name, hostel, block and room so the Store can deliver to you.

## 3. Ordering and pools
- An order becomes final when you place it. It joins the pool that is open. When the pool closes, the delivery fee for everyone in it is fixed by the number of orders in that pool at that moment.
- The price you see for an item is the price when you place the order; later price changes do not change an order already placed.
- A Store may refuse or limit orders when an item is out of stock, the pool is closed, or the Store is paused. You may place up to 3 orders in one pool.

## 4. Prices, fees and payment
Prices are in rupees and include taxes where they apply. The bill shows the item total, the pool delivery fee, the platform fee and any tip. For cash on delivery you pay the amount due to the Store's staff when the order is handed to you. [UPI terms to be added when payments launch.]
