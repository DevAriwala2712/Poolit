## The short version (this is also the wording for the sign-up consent screen)

> Poolit saves your mobile number, name, hostel, block and room, and what you order, so your Store can deliver to you. Your mobile number is used to sign you in and is never sold. Your Store and its staff see your name, room and order. You can download your data or delete your account in the app at any time. By continuing you confirm you are 18 or older and agree to this policy.

## Facts this relies on (from how the system really works)

| What we hold | Why | Who can see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile number (from sign-in) | Sign you in, send you a one-time code | You; Poolit's systems. Not shown to stores. |
| Name, hostel, block, room | Deliver your order | You; the Store that delivers to you (name, block and room on each order) |
| Orders: items, prices, tip, payment method, delivery fee, note, time, status | Run the pool, bills, the Store's accounts | You; the Store; Poolit |
| Cash handed over for cash-on-delivery orders | Reconcile the Store's cash | The Store; Poolit |
| Technical records: errors and server logs | Keep the service working and secure | Poolit's engineers |

Other students never see your name, number or order, only how many orders are in the pool. We do not collect your location, contacts or photos. Store accounts see only their own store's orders.

## Security
Access to data is limited by the database itself: a student can reach only their own data, and a Store only its own store. Backups are encrypted. We test these protections automatically. If a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Children
Poolit is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

## Changes
We will tell you in the app before a change that affects how your data is used.
