## Facts this relies on
- The Store sells; Poolit provides the app, the pooling of orders and the order and cash records. The **Store's own staff deliver** the orders to the student's block and room.
- Students can pay cash on delivery today; the Store's staff collect it and keep it. Poolit does not hold the Store's money. UPI is planned and will change section 5.
- Each delivered order produces a ledger line (goods value, fee, fee type and value used), so the commission can be shown and checked per order. The fee is set per Store by Poolit staff. **Commission versus a fixed charge is not decided**; the draft below keeps both so the lawyer sees the shape.
- The delivery fee a student pays depends on the pool size (₹20, ₹10, ₹5 or free). The platform fee (₹4) is Poolit's. **Who keeps the delivery fee is not decided** (see 4.3).
- The Store sees only its own store's orders. A student's name, block, room and note are shown to the Store to deliver.

## 1. What the Store promises
1. It holds and will keep a valid FSSAI licence or registration and any other licence needed to sell what it lists, and gives Poolit a copy.
2. Every listing is accurate: name, price (MRP is not exceeded), size or weight, ingredients or allergens where relevant, manufacturer, expiry, and country of origin as the law requires.
3. It sells only lawful goods and nothing age-restricted (tobacco, alcohol, etc.) unless Poolit agrees in writing and the law allows.
4. It keeps stock figures honest and does not take orders it cannot fill.
5. Food is stored, packed and handed over safely and within date; damaged or wrong goods are replaced or not charged.

## 2. Orders and delivery
- The Store sets the pool window and closes pools through the app, packs the orders and delivers them by its own staff.
- It treats each order as one customer, hands each order only to the student who placed it, and does not leave unattended food at doors.
- It tells the student if an item is unavailable and corrects the order before delivery.
- Staff who deliver are the Store's people, not Poolit's. The Store is responsible for their conduct, pay and any law that applies to them. [LAWYER: worker and insurance obligations.]

The Store may use a student's name, block, room, note and order only to deliver and to resolve problems with that order.
