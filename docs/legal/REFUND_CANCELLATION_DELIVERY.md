# Refund, Cancellation and Delivery policy (DRAFT for legal review)

## Facts this relies on
- Today the app has **no in-app cancel or refund button**. A student contacts the grievance desk or the Store. Sections marked **(to build)** describe what the policy promises once the feature exists; do not publish a promise the product cannot keep.
- Payment today is cash on delivery, so most refund questions are "do not pay for what you did not get". UPI refunds arrive with payments (Phase 2).
- The pool delivery fee is fixed when the pool closes. Items are priced at the time of order.

## Cancellation
- **Before the pool closes:** you can ask to cancel; the Store removes the order and returns the stock. **(to build: a Cancel button until the pool closes.)**
- **After the pool closes:** the Store has started packing. Cancel only if the Store agrees; otherwise the order is delivered as placed.
- A Store that cannot supply an item cancels that item or the order and tells the student.

## Wrong, missing or damaged items
- Tell the Store's staff at the door; do not accept or pay for what is missing or damaged. For cash on delivery you pay only for what you keep.
- If you find a problem later, report it within **[24 hours]** through the grievance desk with the order number. The Store will [replace, credit or refund] as agreed.

## Delivery
- Store staff deliver to your block and room. Delivery times shown are estimates and depend on the pool and the Store's packing.
- If nobody is available to receive an order, the Store [calls once, waits N minutes, then returns the order: STORE POLICY]. A returned order is not charged.

## Refund method and time
- Cash orders: no payment is taken for items not delivered.
- UPI orders (Phase 2): refunded to the same UPI account within **[N working days]** of approval.
- Refund of the pool delivery fee if the order is not delivered: yes, in full.

## Disputes
See the [Grievance policy](GRIEVANCE_REDRESSAL.md): acknowledgement within 48 hours, resolution within one month.
